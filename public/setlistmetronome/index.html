Setlist Metronome

A metronome that keeps the tempo, time signature, rhythm, and sound for each song. Pick a song in the setlist to switch to its settings. Changes are saved automatically and are still there next time. Free, no sign-up, and it works offline.

    How to use

    1. Add songs – Tap “+ Add song” and type the song title in the box at the top. Each song keeps its own settings.
    2. Set the tempo – Use −5 / −1 / +1 / +5, the slider, type the number, or tap “Tap” in time with the music.
    3. Time signature – Set the top and bottom numbers with − and +. BPM counts the bottom note value.
    4. Rhythm and sound – Choose 4 beat, 8ths, 16ths, triplets, or a shuffle, and a sound. Drums play a simple kick, snare, and hi-hat pattern.
    5. Accents – Tap a beat circle to switch it between accent, normal, and silent.
    6. Practice the setlist – Use “← Prev” and “Next →” to move to the next song. The metronome keeps playing at the new song's settings.

    Privacy

    • Everything stays on your device – The sound is made only in your browser. Your songs and settings are never sent to a server. This app has no accounts, cookies, or ads.
    • Data saved in your browser – Your setlist (song titles, tempo, time signature, rhythm, sound, accents, and volume) is saved in your browser's storage so it is there next time. To work offline, the app is saved there too. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
    • Server logs and page views – This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Standard access logs (such as IP address, time, and the requested page) are kept for operation and security. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies (the page URL, referrer, browser information, and page load time).
